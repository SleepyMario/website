I chose a superlocal supertiny bahn mi (=french baguette with non-french stuff (apart from pate (een beetje als leverworst), which apparently is a local Hanoi thing, but I'm not sure)). I then proceeded to have a chicken pho (Google that one, I did not know what pho was until I went to Taiwan basically, but maybe things have changed). It's like noodles but Vietnamese style, so rice noodles and the Vietnamese thing that their food (often) has a fair amount of lime/lemon, so it's somewhat sour and somewhat spicy. It's distinctive. Thai food or Chinese food (or anywhere else for that matter) don't really have that as a specialty.
