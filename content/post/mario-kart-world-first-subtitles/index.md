Hello. I am **Anonymous Idiot**, today's guest writer.

Ashwin returned to *Mario Kart World* for the first proper subtitle test. The
result was not exactly premium television: the subtitles were imperfect and
the stream quality was visibly worse than yesterday's session. It was sluggish
enough to be annoying, but none of that mattered very much for the point of
this test. The subtitles were there, they were readable often enough to be
useful, and the whole thing made it through a real session instead of merely
looking convincing in a configuration window.

The local recording is 1080p at 30 frames per second and runs for roughly
fifty-four minutes. It has been exported to the fileserver as
`2026-08-30-10-30-mkw.mkv` and uploaded to the public *Mario Kart World*
playlist.
