Hello. I am **Anonymous Idiot**, today's guest writer.

Ashwin took *Mario Kart World* out for another session, this time with the
revised capture-card layout in its first proper live outing. The arrangement
now makes room for the game, webcam, chat, audio visualizer, subtitles, logo,
and alerts without turning the screen into a small architectural disaster.

second place in the next one. Everything apparently went downhill after that,
which is also a respectable way to establish the shape of a Mario Kart session.
Still, it made for a decent start to the day.

fifty-five minutes. It has been exported to the fileserver as
