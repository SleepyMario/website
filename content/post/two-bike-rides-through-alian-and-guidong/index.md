Ashwin is in Vietnam now, and he's been doing some light weights in his room. A few days ago he was still out on the bike in Taiwan. Both rides went fine. They started the same way, out of 大潭 and through 阿蓮 to 龜洞. Where he went from there depended on the day.

**大潭 → 阿蓮 → 龜洞 → 關廟 → 歸人 → 大潭**
